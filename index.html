Questions

Questions, answered

WhyWritten as direct answers and marked up as FAQ data, so Google's AI Overviews and assistants like ChatGPT can quote Tips'NToes word for word.
Where are the Tips'NToes lounges in Oman?

There are four: Muscat Grand Mall in Al Ghubrah, City Centre Muscat in Al Mawaleh (Seeb), Boulevard Boutique Mall in Shatti Al Qurum, and Sohar City Center in Sohar.

Can I get a manicure or pedicure at home?

Yes. Mani@Home brings our nail treatments to your home. Chat with us to check your area and pick a time.

Can I book online?

Yes. Choose a lounge, a treatment and a day at the top of this page and pick a time, or chat with us and we will book it for you.

Do you host kids' spa birthday parties?

Yes. Our Twinkle birthday "Sparty" is a spa-themed party for little ones, with nail polish and pampering for the whole group. Chat with your nearest lounge to plan a date.

How do you keep tools hygienic?

With Client Guard, every client has a personal kit of files, buffers and cuticle tools, so nothing is shared between clients.
