Questions

Manicure & pedicure, answered

Can I book a manicure and pedicure together?

Yes. Choose both when you book and they are scheduled back to back in one visit.

Do you do manicures at home?

Yes. Mani@Home brings our manicure and pedicure to your home. Chat with us to check your area.

Are the tools shared between clients?

No. With Client Guard, every client has her own kit of files, buffers and cuticle tools.

Which lounges offer manicure and pedicure?

All four: Muscat Grand Mall, City Centre Muscat, Boulevard Boutique Mall and Sohar City Center.
