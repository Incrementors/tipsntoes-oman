Questions

Nail extensions & art, answered

Acrylic or gel extensions: which should I choose?

Acrylic is the stronger choice for longer sets; gel feels lighter and more natural. Your technician will help you choose at the start of the appointment.

Can you repair one broken nail?

Yes. Book a nail repair and we fix the single nail without redoing the full set.

Which lounges do nail extensions?

All four Tips'NToes lounges in Muscat and Sohar.
